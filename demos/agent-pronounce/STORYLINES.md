# Episode 2 — storylines

**The one thing:** two teams wired up the same agents with the same plumbing and
built two different ways to work with them. Neither wins; the viewer learns the
difference and meets Ptah.

**Facts only from `COMPARISON.md`.** T3 Code is shown with respect: its own name
and logo to identify it, no mockery, no scoreboard. Same cast as Episode 1
(same robots, same ElevenLabs voices), 45–60 s, 16:9.

## A. Same Cast, Two Shows (recommended)

*Recommended: the viewer already knows the five robots from Episode 1, so the
difference lands as "same actors, different show" in one cut.*

- **Device / form:** one task, two ways · split screen to one.
- **What you see:** one ticket ("add dark mode") goes to the same five robots
  twice. Left, T3 Code's world: a bright studio of separate booths, one robot
  per booth, the user's cursor picking who talks; a robot slides a sticky note
  (`delegate_task`) to another booth. Right, Ptah's temple: the Conductor bangs
  the gavel, robots peel off as lanes, come back with a deliverable stamped on
  disk, and a different-family robot reviews it. Both finish; the halves merge.
- **First 2 s:** the same ticket drops into both halves at once; every robot in
  both worlds turns to it.
- **Beats:** ticket drops → left: you pick a seat → right: the Conductor
  assigns → the delegation moment on each side → the review moment (Ptah's
  family rule) → both ship → card: "Same agents. Two ways to work with them."
  → Ptah end card.
- **Look:** left: clean light studio in T3 Code's palette; right: the Episode 1
  temple. Robots identical in both.
- **Sound:** two music beds that blend into one on the merge; the cast's voices.

## B. The Plumbing

- **Device / form:** the object is the explanation · an endless zoom.
- **What you see:** the camera dives through the floor of both apps into the
  pipes, and they are the same pipes, labelled for real: `codex app-server`,
  Agent SDK, `@cursor/sdk`, ACP, Pi RPC, a local MCP server. Then it rises into
  two very different buildings on top of them.
- **First 2 s:** two app windows side by side; the floor cracks open under both.
- **Beats:** two apps → dive → shared pipes (convergence) → rise left (seats for
  every agent) → rise right (one gavel, lanes, deliverables) → "Same plumbing.
  Different philosophy." → Ptah end card.
- **Look:** 3D cutaway, brass pipes, labels in mono type, warm temple light on
  the Ptah side.
- **Sound:** a low mechanical bed, one riser into the split, voiced labels by
  the robots.

## C. Cribs (bold)

- **Device / form:** reality-TV house tour · cut between two homes.
- **What you see:** the robots give an MTV-Cribs-style tour of the two places
  they work. T3 Code's place: "every one of us gets our own room, and the
  owner can call us from his phone." Ptah's temple: "this is where the magic
  happens — the Conductor hands out the jobs, and Codex can't review Codex."
- **First 2 s:** Codex at a front door, sunglasses on: "Welcome to my crib.
  Both of them."
- **Beats:** cold open → T3 Code tour (seats, threads, phone remote, PRs) →
  Ptah tour (gavel, lanes, deliverables, memory, Tribunal) → "Which one do you
  live in?" "…Both." → Ptah end card.
- **Look:** handheld tour camera, lower-thirds per room, the same robots.
- **Sound:** a Cribs-style hip-hop-lite bed, door chimes, the cast's voices.

## Findings

### Critical

1. **59.6–64.6 s (full frames 61.1, 64.6).** At 61.1 the green crew occupies one body. At 64.6 four blue robots intersect, a white sphere between them. No block is hauled, poles run through torsos, and left faces are blown white. **Fix:** one lashed block per ramp, leader pulling, two followers a body-and-a-half back, extras deleted, face emissive capped, camera rising up the ramp.

2. **65.5 s (sheet 65.1–68.5).** A yellow sphere and a check ring hang beside a sled. A head is cut by the right edge. No inspector and no block. At 66–67 s the ring sits on a robot head. **Fix:** waist-up inspector, seal landing on a block a third of the frame high, hold half a second, crop nothing.

3. **70.6, 71.2, 75.2, 83, 85.6 s.** No email, no API key, no black box. Still on screen: workspace `nx-video`, session titles about this promo and the nx-video tools, two account plan lines, and at 85.6 a real cost figure plus a session id fragment. **Fix:** reshoot four fake sessions, or cover those regions with UI-colored fills.

4. **95 s and 96.5 s.** A grooved plane at partial opacity ghosts Ptah, the pylon and the sofa. Both small robots wear overlapping yellow torus halos. 96.5 s is bare stone with a hard vertical tile seam and no title. **Fix:** one opaque slab, one UV, no seam, rise over about twelve frames, delete the halos, cut to type only when the stone is solid.

### Major

5. **69.5 s, sheet 70.2 and 90.5.** Both heads are sliced on the left into a blown white column. A crate and a blank flag hold the frame. 70.2 s is a white void. 90.5 s leaves both robots as specks. **Fix:** door centered, robots fully in frame, push ending on the first app pixel, return as a medium two-shot.

6. **45.5 s, and 91.3–95 s.** Best 3D frame: scale, glowing eyes, dust. The banded pole reads as a lamp post, the neck is a coil, and there is no beard or body. The speakers are about a tenth of the frame height. Halos at 95 s ruin the reaction. **Fix:** sandstone god with beard and sceptre, pole deleted, small robots about a fifth of the frame on dialogue holds.

7. **10.9 s and 13.5 s.** The landing page is a hard rectangle over a second, brighter, shifted copy, with "Download" cut off. At 13.5 s a green bar, "It runs all of us. Side", covers the app chrome. **Fix:** one full-frame fade. Keep dialogue captions off the product.

8. **71.2–88 s.** Gold italic, no plate, on the UI. "One Command Center" is on a chat transcript at 71.2 s and missing from the real command center at 75.2 s. The chart is unlabeled at 85.6 s; "Track Every Run" arrives at 88 s. "your desktop." at 70.6 s is leftover dialogue. **Fix:** one 72 px label per clip, from the first 0.4 s, on a dark plate in the lower 18%, only on the screen it names.

### Minor

9. **55–58 s.** Faces are large and clear of the caption. Loose cubes and tiny robots litter the mid-ground, and the right half is empty dune. **Fix:** three block stacks, banners, pyramid closer. Split the line into a wide, one moving block, then the stamp.

# Part 1: Engine practices

Paths in this part are relative to `demos/`; citations are source line numbers. Episode 2 inherits Episode 1's engine and adds a generic tour mount (`agent-comparison/notes/SCENE-3D-REPORT.md:3,9`).

- **Renderer and mount.** A singleton `WebGLRenderer` renders at 1920×1080, pixel ratio 1, antialiasing and drawing-buffer preservation. Output is sRGB with Neutral tone mapping, exposure 1, PCF soft shadows; a PMREM `RoomEnvironment` supplies reflections at intensity 0.38. The optional focus chain uses `RenderPass`, Bokeh and output passes; each composition receives a 2D blit from the shared context. `mount(config,canvas)` is entered through `TribunalReady` and registers seek-driven rendering (`agent-comparison/src/scene.js:28-49,58-111,246-310`; `agent-tribunal/notes/SCENE-3D-REPORT.md:9`).
- **Characters.** Load `cute_robot.glb` with `GLTFLoader`, clone the scene per agent, and replace named BODY/GLOW/SCREEN/EYES materials with per-agent Standard materials. Keep a neutral warm shell, coloured ring, dark screen, bright emissive eyes; measure GLB bounds and +X face direction before scale/yaw. Blink via the GLB eye morph; act via rigid hierarchy and time-sampled squash, nod, hop, lean and glow, not clip advancement (`agent-comparison/src/assets.js:12-25,72`; `agent-comparison/src/robot.js:3-7,36-64,71-125,162-196`; `agent-tribunal/notes/FIX-ROUND-2.md:5-10,20-27`).
- **Determinism.** Simulate once per mount at fixed 1/120 s into sampled arrays; render interpolates by seek time. Use seeded hashes for texture variation, idle, blinks and handheld. No `Math.random`, wall clock, timers or `mixer.update` in scene code. One WebGL context avoids composition proliferation (`agent-tribunal/notes/SCENE-3D-REPORT.md:9,94-96`; `agent-comparison/notes/SCENE-3D-REPORT.md:3,185`; `agent-comparison/src/physics.js:296-303`).
- **Camera grammar.** Episode 1 uses speaker-specific 12-frame eased head/chest pushes, wides, over-the-shoulder depth of field, and six-frame chaos cuts (`agent-tribunal/notes/FIX-ROUND-1.md:7-9`; `agent-tribunal/notes/SCENE-3D-REPORT.md:145-153`). Episode 2 uses a 62° tour camera: bounding-sphere framing, keyed/follow shots, 12-frame pushes, slow drift, seeded 0.8°/10 px/1.6 Hz handheld, six-frame whips with smear. Allow room lead-ins and reaction holds; reserve the lower 22% for captions (`agent-comparison/src/cribs-camera.js:1-14,64-70,166-182`; `agent-comparison/notes/SCENE-3D-REPORT.md:139,157`).
- **Lighting.** Temple: warm gold shadow key, restrained cool rim/hemisphere fill, amber practicals, deep shadows. Night: blue key/hemisphere and moon spot. Booth: warm upper-left key, cool rim and fill, shadow cast away from clear text side (`agent-comparison/src/lighting.js:2-20,65-140`; `agent-tribunal/notes/FIX-ROUND-1.md:14`).
- **Materials and sets.** Standard/Physical materials respond to the rig for shell, wood, stone, metal and glass; Basic is used for distant sky cards. Build real architectural openings so daylight enters through windows. Layer seeded canvas textures (oak, brick, sandstone, skyline, labels), plants, lamps, nameplates and gag props. Episode 2 reused the temple and added loft, street and rooms (`agent-comparison/src/robot.js:44-64,207-208`; `agent-comparison/src/set-loft.js:105-106,293-305,352-376`; `agent-comparison/notes/SCENE-3D-REPORT.md:12-16`).
- **Series rules.** Hook at frame 0, plant each visual punchline, give new sets lead-ins and dialogue/reaction gaps, keep vertical-feed legibility and cast/rig/voice continuity (`series/README.md:31,43,57-77`).

- Do **not** claim Episode 3 abandoned the engine. Office imports Episode 2 wall, robot and camera builders; desert uses seeded textures and a shadowed key; Ptah god uses seeded wrap texture and Standard materials (`agent-pronounce/src/set-office.js:14-18,184-241`; `set-desert.js:17-29,160-189`; `ptah-god.js:13-53`). This may be deliberate, but needs its own close-up silhouette/acting review (`agent-pronounce/src/ptah-god.js:56-109`; `agent-comparison/src/robot.js:85-125,182-196`).

Verify hero action/labels stay large in final crop (`agent-pronounce/src/set-office.js:21,203-241`; `set-desert.js:169-174,195-238`; `agent-comparison/notes/SCENE-3D-REPORT.md:14-16,119-139`; `series/README.md:31,62`). ## Decisions

1. Author both skills in existing project `.claude/skills/` and `.agents/skills/`. Copy to `~/.codex/skills/` for guaranteed Codex global install and `~/.grok/skills/` for Grok. Defer disabled OpenCode/Copilot. Use canaries before relying on Antigravity/project discovery.
2. `threejs-scene-craft` should encode renderer/mount invariants, GLB measurement/recolour, deterministic simulation, camera/lighting/set recipes and seek-safe verification. `video-quality-bar` should encode frame-0 hook, 3D+overlay contact sheets, caption-safe area, pacing, audio listening/measurement and asset/runtime checks.
3. Require canary to state loaded file path and quote a rule; mere skill name mention or MCP tool search does not prove loading.
